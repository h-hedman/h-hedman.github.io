Native American Projects & Mentorship

Applied data science projects and long-term technical mentorship conducted in partnership with Native communities and organizations. This work emphasizes capacity building, ethical data practices, and translating analytical rigor into locally meaningful outcomes.

  • Led applied analysis and reporting efforts for tribal public health initiatives, including tobacco-use surveillance and community-facing summaries.
  • Provided sustained technical mentorship to early-career Native students and analysts in data science, epidemiology, and reproducible research workflows.
  • Supported tribal, state, and federal partners in improving the accuracy and consistency of Native American representation in health and administrative data systems.
  • Organized and led professional development and knowledge-sharing initiatives connecting Native students with practitioners across STEM and public-sector roles.

Selected outreach and science communication work is available here.

Note: Due to the sovereign and sensitive nature of many of these collaborations, most datasets and internal reports are not publicly accessible. Details are shared selectively and with appropriate permissions.
