Operational Optimization and Capacity Planning for Transportation Programs

Applied machine learning and optimization using a national operations dataset early development phase

Developing an applied analytics framework using the National Census of Ferry Operators dataset to support planning assessment and system characterization. The focus is on interpretable modeling and practical decision support rather than large scale deployment.

Core Focus Areas

  • Demand and capacity characterization
    Descriptive and predictive modeling to identify patterns in usage capacity and asset utilization using available system attributes.
  • Operational optimization assessment
    Identification of segments with capacity stress underutilized assets and mismatches between system characteristics and observed demand.
  • System archetype clustering
    Grouping systems into comparable operational profiles to support benchmarking and comparison.
  • Resilience oriented indicators
    Construction of composite indicators based on fleet composition accessibility connectivity and redundancy for comparative assessment.
  • Data readiness evaluation
    Assessment of structure completeness and consistency to inform future analytics and survey design.

Planned Outputs

  • Demand and capacity summary
  • Operational optimization assessment
  • System archetype analysis
  • Resilience indicator framework
  • Data readiness appendix

This work is exploratory and iterative. Methods and scope are refined through versioned updates as feasibility and utility are evaluated.
