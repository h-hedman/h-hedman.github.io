U.S. Department of Transportation — Operational Optimization & Capacity Planning

Applied machine learning and optimization to translate a national operations dataset into interpretable planning insights (v1.0 — early development).

Developing an applied analytics framework using the National Census of Ferry Operators (NCFO), a nationwide dataset describing operators, assets, terminals, and route segments. The goal is to produce practical, policy-relevant decision support outputs using tools and infrastructure available in a regulated federal environment — with an emphasis on interpretable models and feasibility assessment rather than production deployment of large-scale AI systems.

All analyses are derived exclusively from publicly available NCFO materials.

What I’m Building (v1.0)

  • Demand and capacity characterization: Lightweight predictive and descriptive modeling to characterize passenger/vehicle demand patterns from asset attributes, network connectivity, operator features, and geographic context (interpretable emphasis).
  • Operational optimization assessment: Identify segments with capacity stress, under-utilized assets, and mismatches between asset capabilities and inferred demand to support comparative planning.
  • System archetype clustering: Unsupervised learning to group systems into operational archetypes for benchmarking and cross-system comparison.
  • Resilience-oriented indicators: Composite indicator framework informed by fleet composition, fuel type, accessibility, multimodal connectivity, and redundancy — designed for comparative assessment, not prescriptive ranking.
  • Data quality and AI readiness: Evaluate missingness, definitional stability, and cross-year consistency to inform future survey design and enable more advanced analytics over time.

Expected Deliverables

  • Demand and capacity characterization summary
  • Operational optimization assessment
  • System archetype analysis (benchmarking framework)
  • Resilience indicator framework
  • Data quality and AI readiness appendix

Note: This is an exploratory analytics framework intended to support planning and assessment. Versioned updates document scope refinements and methodological improvements over time.
