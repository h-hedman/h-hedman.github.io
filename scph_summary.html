Summit County Public Health — County-Level Analytics & Initiatives

Built and maintained applied analytics for a local public health authority in Colorado, spanning surveillance, reporting automation, and operational decision support under privacy and infrastructure constraints.

Pipeline impact: Reduced recurring reporting workflows from hours of manual effort to under 2 minutes end-to-end.

What I Delivered

  • Operational analytics: Produced decision-ready summaries across multiple health outcomes for non-technical and mixed audiences.
  • Automation & reliability: Replaced fragile, manual reporting steps with versioned pipelines and repeatable outputs.
  • Geospatial & equity-oriented analysis: Identified geographic clustering and subpopulations with elevated risk or unmet need.
  • Forecasting & short-horizon planning: Built predictive and time-series models to support local planning under uncertainty.
  • Standardized estimates: Developed consistent estimation and QA approaches to improve comparability across time and reporting cycles.

Many operational datasets and internal reports are not publicly shareable due to governance and privacy requirements. This page summarizes transferable methods and public-facing artifacts.

Methods: geospatial analysis, time-series modeling, predictive modeling, data QA, reporting automation
Tooling: R, Python, SQL, automated reporting pipelines, GIS tooling
