Operational Public Health Analytics (Restricted)

Led applied analytics for a local public health authority in Colorado, delivering spatial, predictive, and decision-support analyses across multiple health outcomes. Work was conducted in a regulated, low-resource environment with non-public data and infrastructure constraints.

Key Contributions

  • Led analytics supporting operational and policy decision-making across multiple health outcomes (not limited to a single program area)
  • Conducted geospatial analysis to identify clusters and subpopulations with elevated risk or unmet need
  • Built predictive models to support short-term planning and local policy decisions under uncertainty
  • Designed and maintained end-to-end analytical pipelines for statistical and machine learning workflows
  • Developed algorithms to standardize biostatistical estimates and inform equitable vaccine allocation
  • Delivered analytics training for mixed technical audiences, including policymakers, clinicians, and public health practitioners

Due to data governance and privacy requirements, most operational artifacts are not publicly shareable. This page summarizes scope and transferable methods.

Methods: geospatial analysis, time-series modeling, predictive modeling, data quality assessment, decision-support analytics
Tools: R, Python, SQL, automated reporting pipelines, GIS tooling
